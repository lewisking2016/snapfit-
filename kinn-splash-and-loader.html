Kinn splash and loading bar

Both are plain SVG and CSS, so they run in any web view and are easy to rebuild natively.

1. Splash screen

Two rings slide together, the lime lens pops, the name draws in, then the tagline rises.

Move together.
MomentTime
Rings slide in and draw0.2 – 1.1 s
Lime lens pops0.95 – 1.55 s
Letters draw, one by one1.2 – 2.15 s
Tagline rises2.1 – 2.7 s

Background Ink #0D1014, rings Paper #F7F8F6, lens and dot Lime #C6F24E. Total about 2.7 seconds. Do not hold it longer than the app needs: if the app is ready sooner, fade out after the lens pops. When Reduce Motion is on, show the finished logo with no movement.

2. Loading bars

Use the progress bar when you know the percentage and the sliding bar when you do not. Dark screens use Lime, and light screens use Ink, because Lime on white is hard to see.
